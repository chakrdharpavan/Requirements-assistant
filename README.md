# Product Adoption Problem

## Problem Statement

A significant number of companies are logging into our application and using our features for the first time. However, after approximately one month, product usage drops significantly and companies are not thoroughly using the product.

## Observed Behavior

- Companies initially log in and explore the application.
- Companies use multiple features during the initial period.
- After approximately one month, product usage decreases.
- Companies are not consistently using the available features.
- Long-term product engagement is lower than expected.

## Business Impact

The decrease in product usage may negatively affect long-term customer engagement and product adoption.

## Business Requirement

The product team needs to understand the reasons behind the decrease in product usage and identify opportunities to improve long-term engagement.

## Questions To Investigate

- Why does product usage decrease after one month?
- Which features are being used initially?
- Which features are not being used after one month?
- What prevents companies from continuing to use the product?
- What improvements could increase long-term product adoption?
