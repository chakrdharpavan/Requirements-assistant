Online Pharmacy Management System 


Business Requirement:- The business requirement document outlines stakeholders, scope, 
Business requirements and constraints etc. The current process completely requires human intervention. 
After bringing out this new feature, the human intervention will decrease and mitigate lot of time & effort

- This document is intended to Admin, pharmacist, delivery agent and pharmacy owner

Business objective:-

A new application should be developed
This will ensure the whole manual process should be covered in one application
After enabling this application, we can mitigate lot of time and human process

REQ1:- The platform shall create user registration and login, where user will register with their emails and afterwards login in to the website=
REQ2:- The platform shall provide a search box to search medicines with their relevant search names
REQ3:- The platform shall enable user to upload prescription 
REQ4:- The platform shall cater required access to pharmacist where they will approve prescriptions and reject prescriptions
REQ5:- The platform shall create a delivery tracking system, where users & admin department can track their delivery 
REQ6:- The system shall handle manage stores, generate reports after purchasing medicines 
REQ7:- The system shall maintain stocks and if item is near by expiry date an notification should show on UI
REQ8:- The system shall send an email after delivery with detailed bill
REQ9:- The system shall create daily sales reports, fast moving products, this data will be sent to admin department
REQ10:- The system shall create a suggestion if the product is not available and say "similar product is available although the company may vary”

Scope:-

Within Scope:-

A user login & registration should be developed
A product grid to showcase all the medicines
Within the product grid a search box be developed where user can search medicines
A tracking system should be enabled 
An inventory management system should be able to develop within the UI
A delivery system should be developed
After user purchasing there medicines an emails should be sent

Out of Scope:-

Discounts if the purchase order goes beyond 1000 rupees
If a person joins with another referral code, an initial order will be 5% discount
Medicine subscription plans

Constraints:-

The product should be delivered within one day
Budget shouldn’t cross limits
All the development script should be kept in one repository

Problem Statement:-

Currently users depend completely on manual intervention. 
Customers have to visit the store physically to purchase medicines, although after developing this application, 
customers can order their medicines online and get them delivered by doorstep.

Success Criteria:-

After bringing out this website our sales may increase to 20%
Customers can rely on our website and happy with our process
Currently only 50 orders are completing every day but after bringing out this app, we can increase daily sales 

Acceptance Criteria:-

User must have their email and password to login
Initially we have to promote our website through social media otherwise customers don’t know about the company
Users can pay cash through UPI or online
